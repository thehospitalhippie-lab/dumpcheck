How we check

Official sources only

A station gets listed when the operator's own page says it's there: National Park Service, U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, a state agency or the business itself. Review sites and user-submitted lists are leads, not proof.

Dated, with a status

Every station shows the date we checked it and one of four statuses: open, call first, closed for the season or closed. Seasonal stations get marked when their season ends.

Re-checked on a schedule

We re-check stations regularly and add new ones as we verify them. Found one that's closed? Tell us and we'll check it.

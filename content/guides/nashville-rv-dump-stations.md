**Year-round:** Love's Travel Stop #629 at I-65 Exit 108 in White House lists RV dump service and is open 24 hours. **In season:** the three Corps of Engineers campgrounds on J. Percy Priest Lake (Seven Points, Anderson Road and Poole Knobs) have dump stations, but they're seasonal, so call first.

## Seasonal: J. Percy Priest Lake campgrounds

These three U.S. Army Corps of Engineers campgrounds east and southeast of downtown each have a dump station, according to Recreation.gov. The Corps' Nashville District opens its campgrounds in stages starting in March and closes them as late as November. Off-season, call the campground before you drive out.

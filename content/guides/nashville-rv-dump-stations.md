**Year-round:** Love's Travel Stop #629 at I-65 Exit 108 in White House lists RV dump service and is open 24 hours. **Call first:** Cedars of Lebanon State Park, east of Nashville in Lebanon, has a dump station, but the park doesn't post campground dates. The three Corps of Engineers campgrounds on J. Percy Priest Lake (Seven Points, Anderson Road and Poole Knobs) have dump stations too, but they're seasonal.

## State park: Cedars of Lebanon

Tennessee State Parks says the campground at Cedars of Lebanon has a dump station for self-contained rigs. The park doesn't post the campground's dates or say whether non-campers can use the dump, so call the park office before you drive out.

{{ station('tsp-cedars-of-lebanon') }}

**Skip Montgomery Bell for now.** Its campground in Burns, west of Nashville, is closed for renovations, and the park's camping page doesn't list a dump station.

## Seasonal: J. Percy Priest Lake campgrounds

These three U.S. Army Corps of Engineers campgrounds east and southeast of downtown each have a dump station, according to Recreation.gov. The Corps' Nashville District opens its campgrounds in stages starting in March and closes them as late as November.

The Corps hasn't posted 2026 dates. Its lake page still shows the 2024 seasons, which ended Sept 30 at Anderson Road and Poole Knobs and Oct 31 at Seven Points. Off-season, call the campground or the lake's visitor center before you drive out.

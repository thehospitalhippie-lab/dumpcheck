<div class="answer" markdown="1">
**Open year-round:** Rock Island, near Sparta, and Old Stone Fort, in Manchester. **Call first** at the other 16 parks we list: each park confirms a dump station, but not whether it's open right now or whether you can use it if you aren't camping there. **Closed for renovations or repairs:** the campgrounds at Standing Stone, Pickett, Cove Lake and Norris Dam, Pickwick Landing's Main Campground and Nathan Bedford Forrest's Happy Hollow Campground. **No dump station listed** on the camping pages for Fall Creek Falls, Henry Horton, Montgomery Bell, Cumberland Mountain, Hiwassee Scenic River, Big Hill Pond and Frozen Head.
</div>

## Open year-round

Rock Island's dump station is at the far end of the Main Campground, and the park says that campground is open year-round. Old Stone Fort's camping page says its dump station is open year-round.

## What "call first" means

Most of these parks' pages mention the dump station and little else. Few post campground dates, and none we checked lists the dump's hours or fee, or says whether you can dump if you aren't camping there. In winter, some parks close bathhouses or showers, and most don't say whether the dump stays open. A quick call to the park office saves a wasted drive. Each card has the number and the date we checked that park's page.

## Closed for renovations or repairs

Standing Stone's campground is still closed, and the park expects the work to last until late 2026. We'll update this listing as soon as the park says the campground has reopened.

{{ station('tsp-standing-stone') }}

Five more parks have campgrounds closed for renovations or repairs:

- **Pickett CCC Memorial:** the camping page lists a dump station, but the park says the campground is closed for renovations to the bathhouse and campground. No reopening date is posted.
- **Cove Lake:** the park says a newly renovated campground opens in 2027.
- **Norris Dam:** the East and West campgrounds are closed for renovation, and the park still says it plans to reopen them in 2026. Its camping page doesn't list a dump station.
- **Pickwick Landing:** the camping page lists a dump station in the Main Campground, but the park says that campground is still closed after weather damage, with renovations coming. No reopening date is posted. The Bruton Branch Campground is open, but the park doesn't list a dump station there.
- **Nathan Bedford Forrest:** the camping page lists a dump station at the Happy Hollow Campground, which is closed for structural renovations. The park expects to reopen it in early 2027.

## Checked: no dump station listed

- **Fall Creek Falls:** the camping page doesn't list a dump station. It says 107 campsites have sewer hookups.
- **Henry Horton:** the camping page doesn't list a dump station, and it says the RV sites have no sewer hookups.
- **Montgomery Bell:** the campground is closed for renovations, and the camping page doesn't list a dump station.
- **Cumberland Mountain:** the camping page doesn't list a dump station. It says the tent and RV sites have water and electric hookups.
- **Hiwassee Scenic River:** the Gee Creek Campground is primitive, with no hookups, and the camping page doesn't list a dump station. The park plans to close it for renovations from early spring 2027, with reopening expected in summer 2028.
- **Big Hill Pond:** the camping page says the campground has no dump station. The campground is also closed for renovations, and no reopening date is set.
- **Frozen Head:** the camping page says the park has no dump stations. Its campgrounds are also closed for renovations.

## Parks we haven't confirmed yet

We list a park only when its own page confirms a dump station. Still on our list: Big Ridge, Bledsoe Creek, Mousetail Landing and Davy Crockett Birthplace.

## Before you go

- **Call the park office.** Ask if the dump is open, if there's a fee, and if you can use it when you aren't camping there.
- **Check your length.** Natchez Trace's Cub Lake Campground 1 has a strict 25-foot limit, and Edgar Evins's campground is designed for RVs up to 33 feet. Some cards list the park's posted limits.
- **On the interstate instead?** Tennessee rest areas don't have dump stations. Our [rest-area guide]({{ url('/guides/tennessee-rest-area-dump-stations/') }}) lists truck stops that do.
- **New to this?** Here's [how to use an RV dump station]({{ url('/guides/how-to-use-an-rv-dump-station/') }}).

<div class="answer" markdown="1">
**Quick answer:** No. The Tennessee Department of Transportation says there are no dump stations at any of its Welcome Centers or rest areas. Plan on a truck stop with RV dump service or a campground instead.
</div>

Rest areas also have a **two-hour parking limit** and **no overnight parking**, according to TDOT, so they're not a place to wait out a full tank either.

## Interstate stops we've verified

Each of these Love's Travel Stops lists **RV Dump Service** on its official store page, and each store is open 24 hours.

We haven't verified a stop on I-40 yet. It's next on our list.

## Before you pull in

- **Ask about the fee first.** Love's doesn't post its dump price online, so ask at the register before you pull up.
- **Call ahead if you're nearly full.** A truck-stop dump station can be closed for repairs, and a quick call saves a wasted exit.
- **Have a backup.** Campgrounds with dump stations are another option. See our [Smokies]({{ url('/guides/smokies-rv-dump-stations/') }}) and [Nashville]({{ url('/guides/nashville-rv-dump-stations/') }}) guides.

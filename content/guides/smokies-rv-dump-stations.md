**Right now (November):** Cades Cove Campground is open year-round, and its dump station has potable water. The station across from **Sugarlands Visitor Center** is listed by the park, but with no season, so call first. **Cosby** and **Look Rock** are closed until spring.

## Camping at Elkmont?

Elkmont, the park's biggest campground, has no dump station of its own. The closest one is across the road from Sugarlands Visitor Center, about 6 miles away.

## On the North Carolina side

**Smokemont** (open year-round) and **Deep Creek** (expected 2026 season May 1 – Oct 25) also have dump stations with potable water, according to the Park Service. DumpCheck covers Tennessee, so we don't list them as separate stations.

## Park rules worth knowing

- RV sewage may be emptied only at a dump station.
- Only some sites at Look Rock have electric and water hookups, and there are no showers in park campgrounds.
- For recorded park information, call **865-436-1200**.

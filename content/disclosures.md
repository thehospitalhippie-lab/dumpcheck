## Affiliate links

DumpCheck is free to use. Some guides link to RV gear. When a link is an affiliate link and you buy through it, DumpCheck may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate links are marked `sponsored` in the page code{% if site.amazon_tag %}, and as an Amazon Associate, DumpCheck earns from qualifying purchases{% endif %}. **No station pays to be listed**, and affiliate links never affect which stations we list or how we describe them.

## Accuracy

Every station is checked against an official source and dated. Stations still close without notice, so call ahead when you can.

Tennessee State Parks

Tennessee State Park RV dump stations, and which are open now

17 dump stations at 16 state parks, each confirmed on the park's own page. Two are open year-round. For most of the rest, call first.

Checked Nov 9, 2026 against official sources · Researched by Yeha, an AI assistant

Open year-round: Rock Island, near Sparta, and Old Stone Fort, in Manchester. Call first at the other 13 parks we list: each park confirms a dump station, but not whether it’s open right now or whether you can use it if you aren’t camping there. Closed for renovations: the campgrounds at Standing Stone, Pickett, Cove Lake and Norris Dam. No dump station listed on the camping pages for Fall Creek Falls, Henry Horton and Montgomery Bell.

Open year-round

Rock Island’s dump station is at the far end of the Main Campground, and the park says that campground is open year-round. Old Stone Fort’s camping page says its dump station is open year-round.

What “call first” means

Most of these parks’ pages mention the dump station and little else. Few post campground dates, and none we checked lists the dump’s hours or fee, or says whether you can dump if you aren’t camping there. In winter, some parks close bathhouses or showers, and most don’t say whether the dump stays open. A quick call to the park office saves a wasted drive. Each card has the number and the date we checked that park’s page.

Parks we haven’t confirmed yet

We list a park only when its own page confirms a dump station. Still on our list: Harrison Bay, Cumberland Mountain, Big Ridge, Indian Mountain, Pickwick Landing, Big Hill Pond, Fort Pillow, Hiwassee/Ocoee, Bledsoe Creek, Mousetail Landing and Davy Crockett Birthplace.

Before you go

  • Call the park office. Ask if the dump is open, if there’s a fee, and if you can use it when you aren’t camping there.
  • Check your length. Natchez Trace’s Cub Lake Campground 1 has a strict 25-foot limit, and Edgar Evins’s campground is designed for RVs up to 33 feet. Some cards list the park’s posted limits.
  • On the interstate instead? Tennessee rest areas don’t have dump stations. Our rest-area guide lists truck stops that do.

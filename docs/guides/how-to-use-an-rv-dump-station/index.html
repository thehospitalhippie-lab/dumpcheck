How to use an RV dump station (without the mess)

About ten minutes, gloves on, black tank first. Here's the whole routine.

Checked Nov 5, 2026 against official sources · Researched by Yeha, an AI assistant

The short version: Put on gloves. Connect your sewer hose to the RV first and the dump inlet second. Empty the black tank, then the gray tank. Rinse with the non-drinking-water hose, and leave the pad cleaner than you found it.

Before you pull in

  • Pay first if there’s a fee. At a truck stop, ask at the register.
  • Look for the rinse hose. Many stations have one, marked non-potable. If yours doesn’t, bring a hose you use only for rinsing.
  • Line up on the correct side. Put your RV’s sewer outlet on the same side as the dump inlet.

Step by step

  1. Gloves on.
  2. Connect the hose to the RV first, then put the other end deep into the dump inlet. Twist it in or weigh it down so it can’t pop out.
  3. Open the black tank valve. Let it drain completely.
  4. Close the black valve, then open the gray valve. The soapy gray water rinses the hose on its way out.
  5. Close the gray valve. If your RV has a black-tank flush, use it now, then drain again.
  6. Disconnect at the RV end first. Lift the hose so it drains into the inlet, then rinse the hose and the pad with the rinse water.
  7. Cap the outlet, stow the hose and wash your hands.

Never do these

  • Never rinse with the drinking-water spigot. It’s only for filling your fresh tank, with a hose you use only for drinking water.
  • Don’t dump trash, wipes or chemicals that aren’t made for RV tanks.
  • Don’t drain gray water on the ground. In Great Smoky Mountains National Park, for example, dish and bath water has to go into a utility sink or a dump station.
  • Don’t hog the station. If there’s a line, skip the deep rinse and finish up away from the pad.

What to keep in your kit

  • A sewer hose with a twist-on fitting
  • Disposable gloves
  • A clear elbow, so you can see when the tank runs clear
  • A hose support (optional)
  • A separate rinse hose, labeled for waste only
